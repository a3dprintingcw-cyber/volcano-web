Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This policy explains what information Volcano Street Food (Rooseveltweg 271, Willemstad, Curaçao) collects when you order on volcanostreetfood.com or in the Volcano Street Food app, and what we do with it. The app shows the same website, so this policy covers both.

What we collect

  • Your name and phone number, so we know whose order it is and can call you about it.
  • Your order: the dishes, your choices and notes, the pickup time, the total and whether you pay by cash, card or online.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, your browser or phone gives us an address to send "your food is ready" messages to. You can turn this off at any time in your browser or phone settings.
  • On your own device: your cart, last order and language choice are kept in your browser so the site remembers them. They do not leave your device.

We do not ask for your email, your address or your location, and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Payments

If you pay online, the payment is handled by Sentoo, a payment provider in Curaçao. Your card or bank details go straight to Sentoo and are never seen or stored by us. We only receive whether the payment succeeded. If you pay at pickup, you pay by cash or card at the counter.

How we use it

  • To prepare your order and have it ready for pickup.
  • To contact you about your order, for example if something is sold out.
  • To keep our sales records and bookkeeping, as the law requires.

We do not sell your information and do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Only Volcano Street Food staff and the people who run the website for us. The website and its data are hosted by Cloudflare. Your order may also be printed on a ticket in our kitchen.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept as long as needed for our bookkeeping. If you want us to remove your name and phone number from past orders, ask us and we will do so, unless the law requires us to keep them.

Children

The site and app are meant for adults. Drinks with alcohol can only be ordered by people aged 18 or older.

Your rights and contact

You can ask us what information we have about you, to correct it or to delete it. Call us at +599 9 565 2266 or come by the restaurant.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update this page and the date at the top.
